Welcome

FidusAchates

The faithful companion: the one who walks alongside, recognises, and warns.

FidusAchates learns the way you use this device, your typing rhythm and pointer gestures, and then quietly keeps watch. It is now installed and learning. Here is what it does, and what it will never do.

The two questions it answers

Identity

Is it still you?

After it has learned your rhythm, it notices if someone else takes over the keyboard, and raises an alarm.

Attribution

Is it still a human?

It tells a person apart from an automation, a script, an AI agent or a remote hand, and labels it. Wanted automation stays quiet.

What it will never do

  • ×It never records what you type. Only the rhythm and motion, never the letters, words, passwords or content.
  • ×It never uses the network. Nothing leaves this machine; the program refuses to open a network connection at all.
  • ×It never acts against you. It flags and explains; it does not lock you out, log you off, or block anything.
  • ×It never watches someone else without consent. Observing another person needs their explicit agreement first.

How you will see it

A small indicator sits in your system's bar: the menu bar on macOS, the notification area on Windows, the top bar on GNOME. A coloured square shows its state, and a switch lets you pause it at any moment.

Calm. It recognises you; nothing unusual.
Watching. Something changed; it is gathering evidence.
Alarm. It thinks the person or the control has changed. Open the console to see exactly why.

While it learns

It does not recognise you on day one. It measures how long it needs on this machine and shows the progress in its console (opened from the indicator). Until then it holds back rather than cry wolf.

Your data stays yours

The only things kept on disk are statistical templates, sealed at rest with a key held in your session keyring, never written in clear by default. If you ever copy your home folder elsewhere, those templates travel sealed. See docs/05-PRIVACY.md and docs/10-SECURITY-REVIEW.md in the project.

One honest warning

To read your rhythm, this tool reads your input device. It therefore technically has the power of a keylogger. It is built never to use it that way, and that is checked by automated tests, but the power exists. Keep it only if you are willing to read the code, or to trust someone who has. You can remove it at any time, and pause it instantly from the indicator.

FidusAchates is a local, explainable research tool for continuous authentication. Open the console from the indicator to see every decision, term by term. This page opened once, on first install.
